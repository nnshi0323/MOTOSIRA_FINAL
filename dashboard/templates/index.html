REAL vs DEMO mode

REAL: the Raspberry Pi records with the INMP441; results come from the model trained on your real, mechanic-verified recordings. DEMO: a simulated device on this PC produces synthetic engine sounds and a model trained on a simulated dataset classifies them. DEMO shows the complete pipeline working; it is never a real diagnosis. Real microphone audio is only ever judged by the REAL model.

Running a diagnosis

  • Select the motorcycle model (Honda TMX 125 Alpha is the primary platform).
  • Place the INMP441 about 30 cm from the engine block; start the engine and hold the target RPM.
  • Press START here or on the Pi's touchscreen. The device records 3 seconds, filters the sound, extracts MFCC features and the Random Forest classifies them.
  • Read the result first: condition, confidence and severity. "CHECK NOISE" or "low confidence" means re-record.

Conditions

Normal · Valve ticking (severity 1) · Misfire (severity 2) · Knocking (severity 3). MOTOSIRA identifies conditions from sound only and does not connect to the motorcycle's electrical system.

Reports

Every session is saved automatically (and on the Pi's microSD). Open any row in History for its report; "Save PDF" downloads it; "Export CSV" downloads all sessions.
